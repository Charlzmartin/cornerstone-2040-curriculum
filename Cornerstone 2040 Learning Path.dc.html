Rooted in Cornerstone 2040

Nineteen sessions to root your walk in Christ.

This curriculum walks through Cornerstone 2040 chapter by chapter — the church’s pastoral vision for a Christ-centered future. Each session is built for a full hour together: an opening question, teaching content, Scripture and discussion, and a closing application and prayer — whether you’re studying alone or leading a group.

How to use this: This page is a self-contained study — open it from any browser, on desktop or phone, no login required. Share the link with your class or small group so everyone studies from the same page; each person’s own completion progress saves privately on their own device. Leaders can toggle the Leader Notes panel on each session from the Tweaks controls when preparing to teach.
