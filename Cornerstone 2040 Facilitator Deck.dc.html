CORNERSTONE 2040
A Six-Session Facilitator Curriculum
FOR IN-PERSON CLASSES OR ZOOM GROUPS · 60 MINUTES PER SESSION
SESSION 1 · UNIT 1
Foundations of Identity
Chapters 1–4: A Steward's Declaration · The Cornerstone Identity · Our Mission · Our Vision
WELCOME
5 min
SCRIPTURE
15 min
DISCUSSION
15 min
ACTIVITY
15 min
PRAYER
10 min
SCRIPTURE FOCUS
“Unless the Lord builds the house, the builders labor in vain.”
Psalm 127:1
“You also, like living stones, are being built into a spiritual house.”
1 Peter 2:5
“As the Father has sent me, I am sending you.”
John 20:21
“He who began a good work in you will carry it on to completion.”
Philippians 1:6
BIG IDEA
Cornerstone belongs to Christ. Our identity, mission, and vision all flow from Him — not from a pastor, a style, or a method.
  • We are stewards, not owners, of doctrine, relationships, and influence.
  • Our identity is found in Christ alone, not a worship style or shared history.
  • Our mission: help people know, trust, and follow Jesus — and lead others to do the same.
  • Our vision: a church that treasures Christ and outlives its leaders.
SMALL-GROUP DISCUSSION · BREAKOUT GROUPS OF 3–4
15 minutes · in-person: cluster chairs · Zoom: use Breakout Rooms
1
Where do you treat something entrusted to you as if it belonged to you alone?
2
Which identity marker are you most tempted to treasure above Christ?
3
What's one "next faithful step" you sense Christ inviting you to take?
ROLE-PLAY ACTIVITY · PAIRS · 15 MINUTES
"What's your church like?"
Pair up. Partner A plays a curious neighbor asking, "What's your church like?" Partner B answers in a way that centers Christ — not programs, style, or size. Switch roles and repeat.
Debrief: What was hard about resisting the urge to describe programs first?
PRAYER · 10 MINUTES
In your pair or group, thank God aloud for one specific way He has been "building the house" in this church's history — then pray for each other's next faithful step.
TAKEAWAY
We are stewards, not owners — and our identity, mission, and vision are found in Christ alone.
SESSION 2 · UNIT 2
Values and the Path of Discipleship
Chapters 5–8: Our Core Values · The Cornerstone Path · The Fruit of the Path · Released to Ministry
WELCOME
5 min
SCRIPTURE
15 min
DISCUSSION
15 min
ACTIVITY
15 min
PRAYER
10 min
SCRIPTURE FOCUS
“Whatever you do, do it all in the name of the Lord Jesus.”
Colossians 3:17
“I am the way and the truth and the life.”
John 14:6
“This is to my Father's glory, that you bear much fruit.”
John 15:8
“To each one the manifestation of the Spirit is given for the common good.”
1 Corinthians 12:7
BIG IDEA
Cornerstone's values are shown by what we practice, not what we print. Discipleship is a lifelong path with Christ that bears fruit and releases every believer into ministry.
  • Values: treasure Christ, submit to the Word, believe before we obey, stay with Christ, grow together, serve generously, multiply disciples.
  • The Path is not a ladder — "You Are Here" meets you honestly in your season.
  • Fruit is Christ becoming visible in us, not perfection.
  • Every believer is gifted by the Spirit; there are no spectators.
SMALL-GROUP DISCUSSION · BREAKOUT GROUPS OF 3–4
15 minutes
1
Which of the seven values is currently weakest in your life?
2
Where are you on the Path right now — believing, growing quietly, waiting, suffering, serving, leading?
3
What gift has God given you that you haven't yet offered the church?
SCENARIO ACTIVITY · PAIRS · 15 MINUTES
"You Are Here" interview
In pairs, take turns interviewing each other: "Where are you on the Cornerstone Path right now, and what would faithfulness look like there?" Listen without trying to fix; simply reflect back what you heard.
PRAYER · 10 MINUTES
Pray by name for your partner's current season on the Path.
TAKEAWAY
Values are practiced, not printed. The Path is Christ, and every believer has a ministry.
SESSION 3 · UNIT 3
Leadership and Culture
Chapters 9–10: Leadership as Servanthood · A Culture of Truth and Grace
WELCOME
5 min
SCRIPTURE
15 min
DISCUSSION
15 min
ACTIVITY
15 min
PRAYER
10 min
SCRIPTURE FOCUS
“Whoever wants to become great among you must be your servant.”
Matthew 20:26
“Speaking the truth in love, we will grow into the mature body of Christ.”
Ephesians 4:15
BIG IDEA
Leadership is servanthood proven by character. A healthy culture holds truth and grace together.
  • The towel comes before the title — leadership begins at Jesus' feet.
  • Gift attracts attention; character sustains ministry.
  • Truth without love is a weapon; love without truth is a lie.
  • We refuse to "play church" and make room for repentance.
SMALL-GROUP DISCUSSION · BREAKOUT GROUPS OF 3–4
15 minutes
1
Where are you tempted to hold something tightly out of fear no one else will do it "right"?
2
Is it harder for you to speak truth, or to speak it in love?
3
Where in your life are you "playing church" — looking fine but unchanged underneath?
ROLE-PLAY ACTIVITY · GROUPS OF 3 · 15 MINUTES
Practicing a hard conversation
In groups of three, one person shares a real or realistic scenario needing a hard conversation. Practice responding with "I was wrong, please forgive me" instead of "I'm sorry you felt that way." Rotate roles so everyone practices.
PRAYER · 10 MINUTES
Pray for courage to have one honest conversation this week.
TAKEAWAY
The towel comes before the title. Truth and love must stay together.
SESSION 4 · UNIT 4
Spiritual Rhythms
Chapters 11–14: Prayer as Dependence · Biblical Community · Generosity as a Way of Life · Worship That Transforms
WELCOME
5 min
SCRIPTURE
15 min
DISCUSSION
15 min
ACTIVITY
15 min
PRAYER
10 min
SCRIPTURE FOCUS
“Apart from me you can do nothing.”
John 15:5 — Prayer
“By this everyone will know you are my disciples, if you love one another.”
John 13:35 — Community
“Freely you have received; freely give.”
Matthew 10:8 — Generosity
“God is spirit, and his worshipers must worship in the Spirit and in truth.”
John 4:24 — Worship
BIG IDEA
Prayer, community, generosity, and worship are the rhythms that sustain a disciple's life with Christ.
  • Prayer is dependence — our first response, not our last resort.
  • We were never meant to walk alone.
  • Generosity is a life of open hands, not just an offering.
  • Worship must move from Sunday singing into Monday obedience.
SMALL-GROUP DISCUSSION · FOUR GROUPS, ONE RHYTHM EACH
15 minutes · each group reports back one insight
PRAYER GROUP
Is prayer your first response or your last resort?
COMMUNITY GROUP
Who knows the real you, past "I'm fine"?
GENEROSITY GROUP
What has God placed in your hands that you could give this week?
WORSHIP GROUP
Where does your worship stop at emotion and never reach obedience?
SCENARIO ACTIVITY · PAIRS · 15 MINUTES
Noticing and responding to a need
In pairs, Partner A describes a real or hypothetical need (a lonely neighbor, a financial strain, a tired volunteer). Partner B practices responding with one concrete, generous next step — not "I'll pray about it."
PRAYER · 10 MINUTES
As a whole group, pray through each rhythm in turn — dependence in prayer, connection in community, open hands in generosity, and surrender in worship.
TAKEAWAY
Apart from Christ we can do nothing — and no one should walk, give, or worship alone.
SESSION 5 · UNIT 5
Multiplying Disciples
Chapters 15–16: Every Member a Disciple · Protecting the Culture
WELCOME
5 min
SCRIPTURE
15 min
DISCUSSION
15 min
ACTIVITY
15 min
PRAYER
10 min
SCRIPTURE FOCUS
“Go and make disciples of all nations.”
Matthew 28:19
“Stand firm. Let nothing move you. Always give yourselves fully to the work of the Lord.”
1 Corinthians 15:58
BIG IDEA
The mission belongs to the whole church; every disciple has a next step and helps someone else take theirs. Culture is protected by what we repeatedly practice.
  • "Working together, we exist to make disciples" is everyone's calling, not a staff job.
  • Every disciple has a next faithful step and someone to help.
  • Culture is formed by repetition — what we tolerate, we teach.
  • Convictions must be protected; preferences may be released.
SMALL-GROUP DISCUSSION · BREAKOUT GROUPS OF 3–4
15 minutes
1
If someone asked, "Who are you helping follow Jesus?" could you give a name?
2
Where do you rely on attendance in place of active discipleship?
3
What's one Cornerstone story of God's faithfulness you could tell someone newer?
ROLE-PLAY ACTIVITY · PAIRS · 15 MINUTES
A disciple-making conversation
Partner A plays a newer believer with a real question. Partner B practices listening, pointing to Scripture, and offering to walk alongside — without needing to have all the answers. Switch roles.
PRAYER · 10 MINUTES
Pray by name for the person each of you named as someone you're discipling or could disciple.
TAKEAWAY
Every member is a disciple-maker. Protect convictions; release preferences.
SESSION 6 · UNIT 6
Legacy and the Future
Chapters 17–19: Succession as Stewardship · Measuring Faithfulness · Kingdom Outcomes
WELCOME
5 min
SCRIPTURE
15 min
DISCUSSION
15 min
ACTIVITY
15 min
PRAYER
10 min
SCRIPTURE FOCUS
“Entrust to reliable people who will also be qualified to teach others.”
2 Timothy 2:2
“Those who have been given a trust must prove faithful.”
1 Corinthians 4:2
“Now to him who is able to do immeasurably more than all we ask or imagine.”
Ephesians 3:20-21
BIG IDEA
We measure faithfulness, not just numbers; we prepare the next generation; and we plan with open hands, trusting Christ with Cornerstone's future.
  • Every leader serves a season; succession begins with discipleship.
  • We measure faithfulness — "Am I closer to Christ than a year ago?"
  • Faithful leaders prepare people, not replacements.
  • We plan with open hands, trusting Christ with more than we can ask or imagine.
SMALL-GROUP DISCUSSION · BREAKOUT GROUPS OF 3–4
15 minutes
1
Are you more focused on being needed, or on preparing someone else to be ready?
2
"Am I closer to Christ than I was a year ago?" — answer honestly.
3
What's one sentence you'd want future generations of this church to say was true of you?
ROLE-PLAY ACTIVITY · PAIRS · 15 MINUTES
The handoff conversation
In pairs, one person shares a responsibility they currently carry (at church, work, or home) and practices explaining it clearly enough that their partner could step in and help. Discuss: what makes releasing responsibility hard?
CLOSING PRAYER · 10 MINUTES
Write a one-sentence prayer for this church's future. Share aloud (or in the Zoom chat), then close together in shared prayer.
TAKEAWAY
Every leader serves a season. Faithfulness — not size — is the measure that matters.
