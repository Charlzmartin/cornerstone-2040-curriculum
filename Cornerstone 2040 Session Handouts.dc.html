SESSION 1 · UNIT 1
Foundations of Identity
Cornerstone 2040
SCRIPTURE
Psalm 127:1 · 1 Peter 2:5 · John 20:21 · Philippians 1:6
BIG IDEA
Cornerstone belongs to Christ. Our identity, mission, and vision all flow from Him — not from a pastor, a style, or a method.
DISCUSSION QUESTIONS
1. Where do you treat something entrusted to you as if it belonged to you alone?
2. Which identity marker are you most tempted to treasure above Christ?
3. What's one "next faithful step" you sense Christ inviting you to take?
ACTIVITY
Role-play, pairs: Practice answering "What's your church like?" in a way that centers Christ, not programs or style.
TAKEAWAY
We are stewards, not owners — and our identity, mission, and vision are found in Christ alone.

SESSION 2 · UNIT 2
Values and the Path of Discipleship
Cornerstone 2040
SCRIPTURE
Colossians 3:17 · John 14:6 · John 15:8 · 1 Corinthians 12:7
BIG IDEA
Cornerstone's values are shown by what we practice, not what we print. Discipleship is a lifelong path with Christ that bears fruit and releases every believer into ministry.
DISCUSSION QUESTIONS
1. Which of the seven values is currently weakest in your life?
2. Where are you on the Path right now — believing, growing, waiting, suffering, serving, leading?
3. What gift has God given you that you haven't yet offered the church?
ACTIVITY
Scenario, pairs: Interview each other — "Where are you on the Path right now, and what would faithfulness look like there?"
TAKEAWAY
Values are practiced, not printed. The Path is Christ, and every believer has a ministry.

SESSION 3 · UNIT 3
Leadership and Culture
Cornerstone 2040
SCRIPTURE
Matthew 20:26 · Ephesians 4:15
BIG IDEA
Leadership is servanthood proven by character. A healthy culture holds truth and grace together.
DISCUSSION QUESTIONS
1. Where are you tempted to hold something tightly out of fear no one else will do it "right"?
2. Is it harder for you to speak truth, or to speak it in love?
3. Where in your life are you "playing church" — looking fine but unchanged underneath?
ACTIVITY
Role-play, groups of 3: Practice a hard conversation, saying "I was wrong, please forgive me" instead of "I'm sorry you felt that way."
TAKEAWAY
The towel comes before the title. Truth and love must stay together.

SESSION 4 · UNIT 4
Spiritual Rhythms
Cornerstone 2040
SCRIPTURE
John 15:5 · John 13:35 · Matthew 10:8 · John 4:24
BIG IDEA
Prayer, community, generosity, and worship are the rhythms that sustain a disciple's life with Christ.
DISCUSSION QUESTIONS (by assigned rhythm group)
1. Prayer — Is prayer your first response or your last resort?
2. Community — Who knows the real you, past "I'm fine"?
3. Generosity — What has God placed in your hands that you could give this week?
4. Worship — Where does your worship stop at emotion and never reach obedience?
ACTIVITY
Scenario, pairs: Practice responding to a real or hypothetical need with one concrete, generous next step.
TAKEAWAY
Apart from Christ we can do nothing — and no one should walk, give, or worship alone.

SESSION 5 · UNIT 5
Multiplying Disciples
Cornerstone 2040
SCRIPTURE
Matthew 28:19 · 1 Corinthians 15:58
BIG IDEA
The mission belongs to the whole church; every disciple has a next step and helps someone else take theirs. Culture is protected by what we repeatedly practice.
DISCUSSION QUESTIONS
1. If someone asked "Who are you helping follow Jesus?" could you give a name?
2. Where do you rely on attendance in place of active discipleship?
3. What's one Cornerstone story of God's faithfulness you could tell someone newer?
ACTIVITY
Role-play, pairs: Practice a disciple-making conversation — listening, pointing to Scripture, offering to walk alongside.
TAKEAWAY
Every member is a disciple-maker. Protect convictions; release preferences.

SESSION 6 · UNIT 6
Legacy and the Future
Cornerstone 2040
SCRIPTURE
2 Timothy 2:2 · 1 Corinthians 4:2 · Ephesians 3:20-21
BIG IDEA
We measure faithfulness, not just numbers; we prepare the next generation; and we plan with open hands, trusting Christ with Cornerstone's future.
DISCUSSION QUESTIONS
1. Are you more focused on being needed, or on preparing someone else to be ready?
2. "Am I closer to Christ than I was a year ago?" — answer honestly.
3. What's one sentence you'd want future generations of this church to say was true of you?
ACTIVITY
Role-play, pairs: Practice a "handoff" conversation, explaining a responsibility clearly enough for your partner to step in.
TAKEAWAY
Every leader serves a season. Faithfulness — not size — is the measure that matters.
